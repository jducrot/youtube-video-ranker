- **Claude and Lovable (Tutorial Stack).** A short, thin demo that mostly promotes a Lovable deal.
- **Every Claude 2.0 Concept Explained, 9 NEW Claude Mods, Claude 2.0 is INSANE.** Useful if you want to follow Claude's feature updates, but none is about building websites. Treat their scores as roundup scores.

## 8. Limits

- **Relevance is not scored.** The numeric score counts checkable claims. I corrected for this by re-ranking on-topic videos, but that was my judgment on an inferred goal.
- **Sample only.** 21 of 50 key points were checked (23 searches). The other 29 are opinions, creator results, promo codes, or were outside the budget of about 20. Creators' own results (build times, traffic, income) cannot be verified.
- **Confidence is low** for the Tristen O'Brien, Nate Herk (skill) and Lovable videos.
- **Judgment calls:** which points count as core, and the K12 split, were mine. The Vercel/GitHub penalty depends on the "core" label I set before checking.
- **Time-sensitive sources.** Most checks relied on Anthropic and vendor pages dated October to November 2026. Plans and dates change.
- Transcripts miss screen demos, and auto-captions can garble numbers. No instructions aimed at an AI were found in any transcript.
- Fact-checks were done by Sonnet subagents. The inventories were extracted by Haiku and all quotes passed `verify-quotes`.
